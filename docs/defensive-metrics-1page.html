Defensive metrics: Cressey

What the defender gets, and what is and is not measured. Numbers come from the evaluation runs in the repo.

Time saved for the analyst

Not measured. The repo has no baseline of analyst time. What is measured is machine time: time to stop a call, p50 617 ms with live Jev and the calls back to back (236 ms at the stage's pace, one run); three fix guides in 9 to 14 s.

Summary

Measured: precision on the team's synthetic set (0 false holds, 8/8 incidents held) and machine speed. Not measured: analyst time saved. Coverage is a hand mapping, and the OWASP corpus is not loaded.
